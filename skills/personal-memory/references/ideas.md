# Idea capture reference

Capture the owner's actual idea, preserving uncertainty and alternatives. The title identifies the concept; body retains the owner's details and constraints. A concise summary supports retrieval but never replaces the source. Tags should describe the subject, not invent a business category or assessment. Do not turn a speculative idea into a committed task, scheduled event, purchase or business fact.

An explicit "do not save this" outranks automatic capture. A quoted example, a question about someone else's idea, and an assistant-generated suggestion are not new owner ideas unless the owner adopts them. Ask if ownership or meaning is unclear. When the owner elaborates on an existing idea, search for it; update only after reading the exact current id/version and preserving unrelated details. Different ideas remain different records.

When recalling ideas, cite titles and dates, distinguish the owner's words from your analysis, and avoid retrieving unrelated records. Do not perform background model calls to rewrite every idea. Suggest refinement conversationally; record the refinement only when the owner states or adopts it. All writes pass the same server tool validation and explicit success acknowledgement.
