# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information or repeatedly disrupt project discussions.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Report concerns through GitHub's private reporting tools or a private maintainer channel. Do not post private conversations or identifying details in public issues. Maintainers should handle reports fairly and disclose only what is necessary.
